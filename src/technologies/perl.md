[Perl](https://www.perl.org/) is a programming language first developed in 1987. While it's not used much in newer projects, it is still widely used in legacy projects with large, complicated codebases. I experienced working in Perl for around 2 years while working for the Faculty of Informatics of Masaryk University in Brno. I was part of a team working on the University's Informational System, used by dozens of institutions across the country by hundreds of thousands of students and professors on a daily basis. Working on such a complex system taught me important core principles, such as emphasizing code readability and sustainability in high-usage production environments, mitigating the effects of long-term tech debt, and working directly with end users. While it isn't as robust as some of my favorite Javascript frameworks, I ended up really liking my time working in Perl!
