[Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) is fullstack React-based framework, allowing developers to have a shared codebase for the front-end and back-end sections of a project. While I generally prefer working with separate codebases, using a fullstack framework definitely has its benefits. I have worked with Next extensively while working at the Oáza Olomouc non-profit, developing the digital archive Trezor. I found the framework to be fairly mature and easy to understand, allowing for quick iteration and using well-integrated ecosystem plugins and third-party tools.
