Trezor is a non-profit project I'm working on; it's a Czech digital archive of academic works with queer topics that are in danger of being censored. I'm the lead developer of this project, designing the entire architecture and implementing the server, website, and back-end of the system. Public access to the website is currently limited, but I'm working on writing a short case study about it.
