HKSkins is a website compiling an organized list of user-created skins for the games *Hollow Knight (2017)* and *Hollow Knight: Silksong (2025)*. The website runs on Gatsby.js and utilizes GraphQL to display and sort individual skins based on metadata in JSON files in the project's filesystem. HKSkins is by far my most visited website, with around 3&nbsp;000 visitors a day.
