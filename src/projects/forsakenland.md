Forsakenland is a work-in-progress grimdark sci‑fi game inspired by the world of Warhammer 40k. On this project, I worked as the lead freelance world designer, creating the setting of Gylesian‑3C, a desert planet. I've created about 25 pages of notes so far; a sample can be seen by clicking the button below. The project is currently on hiatus.
