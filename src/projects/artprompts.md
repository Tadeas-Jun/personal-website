Art Prompts is one of my largest personal projects -- it's a fullstack web application designed inspire artists to draw every day. The project includes a website, web app (600+ registered users), internal API, a Discord bot (7k+ servers), and more. Implementing the project included longterm project management, payment gate integration, OAuth2 implementation, and other complex features.
